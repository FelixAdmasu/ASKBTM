About & Mission

Family Unity First

We believe a child's well-being should never come at the cost of separation from parents. Daytime care allows mothers to work while ensuring children return home each evening.

  • Morning reception & check-in
  • Evening happy reunification

Holistic Nutrition

Nutrition is the foundation of cognitive and physical development. We provide balanced, fortified meals daily so hunger never interferes with a child's dream or learning.

  • Breakfast, hot lunch & dinner
  • Pediatric health monitoring

Maternal Empowerment

A mother's economic independence is the greatest safeguard against poverty. We provide micro-loans, vocational training, and business mentorship for self-reliance.

  • Revolving micro-credit loans
  • Skills training in coffee & sewing

“No child should ever be left behind while their mother works. No mother should lose her child to poverty.”

Sister Sirgutemikael Teklu (MSc Public Health) — Founder & Executive Director
