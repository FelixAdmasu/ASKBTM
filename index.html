Aba Selama Kesate Brihan Terado Mehaber (ASKBTM)

We are dedicated to the belief that family unity is paramount in a child's upbringing. Our founder, Mother Sirgutemikael Teklu, emphasizes that the well-being of children should never come at the expense of their connection to their parents. We strive to create an environment where children can thrive while remaining with their families.

Our Vision

We aspire to break the cycle of poverty by ensuring that every child grows up in a nurturing family environment supported by a caring community.

  • Family Unity: Strengthen the parent-child bond through day-time care that never replaces families.
  • Holistic Development: Address physical, cognitive and emotional growth during the most formative years.
  • Inclusive Community: Build a support network where neighbours, volunteers and local businesses work together for children's success.

Day Care Center in Legatofo

To realize this vision, ASKBTM operates a Day Care Center in Legatofo, offering a safe and nurturing space for children. Our comprehensive approach includes:

  • Morning Reception: Children are warmly welcomed each morning, setting a positive tone for the day ahead.
  • Nutritious Meals: We provide balanced meals—breakfast, lunch, and dinner—to support the children's physical and mental development.
  • Full-Day Care: Engaging educational and recreational activities are designed to foster overall development.
  • Evening Reunification: Children are reunited with their parents, reinforcing family bonds.
  • Parent Assistance: We offer guidance, financial assistance, and resources to help families thrive.

Why We Do It

Children flourish when surrounded by love, stability and opportunity. Unfortunately, economic hardship often forces parents to choose between work and quality childcare. ASKBTM exists to make that choice unnecessary.

  • Child Well-Being: Protect children from neglect, malnutrition and unsafe environments.
  • Parental Empowerment: Give caregivers the freedom to work or study, knowing their children are safe.
  • Community Impact: Reduce school drop-outs and improve long-term socio-economic outcomes.

Together we create a future where every child can dream, learn and grow while staying close to the people who matter most.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about our work, programmes and how you can get involved.

Visit our centre or complete the online pre-registration form. Our staff will then schedule an orientation and help with the paperwork.

To enroll your child in our day-care program, we invite you to visit our center in person, where you can get a feel for our environment and
meet our caring staff. Alternatively, you can complete the convenient online pre-registration form available on our website. Once we receive your information,
our dedicated staff will reach out to schedule an informative orientation session, guiding you through all necessary paperwork and answering any questions you may have about our program. We look forward to welcoming you and your child to our community!
Our services operate on a sliding scale model, designed to accommodate the diverse financial situations of families. We encourage those who are able to contribute to do so, as their support helps sustain our programs. For families facing financial difficulties, we offer scholarships that are generously funded by our dedicated donors, ensuring that everyone has access to the resources they need.
We currently provide nurturing care for children ranging from 6 months to 6 years of age. Our tailored programs are designed to support the developmental needs of young learners. Additionally, we are excited to announce that we will soon be introducing after-school tutoring services for older children, allowing us to expand our support to a wider age range. Stay tuned for more details on this upcoming offering!
If you’re interested in making a difference through volunteering, we’d love to have you on board! To get started, please fill out our volunteer form, which will give us insight into your skills and availability. Alternatively, you can reach out to us directly at volunteer@askbtm.org. Once we receive your information, we will carefully match your unique talents with our current needs. Additionally, we will provide you with a comprehensive orientation to ensure you feel prepared and confident in your role. Thank you for considering this opportunity to contribute to our community!
Our program is designed to provide a rich and engaging environment for children, seamlessly blending play-based learning with essential early literacy and numeracy skills. Each day begins with a vibrant circle time, where children gather to share their thoughts and participate in interactive discussions. Throughout the day, they explore themed learning centers that inspire curiosity and creativity, allowing them to dive into hands-on activities that spark their imagination.

In addition to our focus on academics, we incorporate creative arts, enabling children to express themselves through various mediums, from painting to music. Outdoor exploration is also a vital part of our curriculum, encouraging children to connect with nature and foster their physical development through active play.

Nutrition is a priority, and we serve wholesome meals that fuel their bodies and minds. After a busy morning of learning and exploration, children enjoy a restful nap time to recharge. At the end of the day, we value connection with families; thus, we take time for reflection during pick-up, sharing highlights and insights about each child's day. This thoughtful approach ensures a well-rounded experience that nurtures their growth and development.
To ensure the utmost safety and health of the children in our care, we take a comprehensive approach. All staff members are not only first-aid certified but are also rigorously vetted to guarantee a safe environment. Our facility boasts secure entry systems and is under continuous surveillance through CCTV monitoring, providing an extra layer of security. We prioritize cleanliness with routine sanitation procedures throughout the center and have an on-call pediatric nurse available to address any health concerns. Additionally, we maintain a favorable educator-to-child ratio of 1:6, allowing for personalized attention and care, while strictly adhering to national child-care guidelines to create a nurturing and safe atmosphere for all children.
