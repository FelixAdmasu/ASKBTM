### 🎯 Mission
To empower children and mothers with accessible resources, structured support, and practical tools for family growth.

### 🌱 Vision
A future where every child and mother has equitable access to knowledge, care, and opportunity.

---

### 💡 Features
- ✅ Digital resource management for children & mothers  
- ✅ Community support platform  
- ✅ Easy-to-access information hub  
- ✅ QR code integration for smart tracking
