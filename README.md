### 🎯 Mission & Vision

<div align="justify">

**Mission**  
To empower children and mothers by providing accessible, structured, and culturally relevant resources — practical tools, knowledge, and support systems that strengthen family bonds and build lasting resilience.

**Vision**  
A future where **no child** and **no mother** faces barriers to education, healthcare, opportunity, or community — a world of equity, dignity, and shared growth.

</div>
