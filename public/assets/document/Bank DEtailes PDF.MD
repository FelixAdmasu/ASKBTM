### Our Commitment  
In Legatafo, many mothers rise before dawn to provide for their families. Limited resources often force impossible choices between income, childcare, and education.  

Freminatos Children and Mothers’ Resource Center offers a proven solution: comprehensive daytime care, nutrition, early education, and maternal economic empowerment—ensuring no child is separated from their mother due to poverty.  

**This is not assistance. This is sustainable justice.**

### A Letter from the Executive Director  
Sister Sirgut Emikael, MSc Public Health, Founder & Executive Director  

For twelve years I have walked the red-dirt paths of Legatafo at 5 a.m., listening to mothers count coins they do not have.  
I have held infants while their mothers chose between buying teff or paying rent.  
I have watched six-year-olds fall asleep in class because hunger is louder than the alphabet.  

In 2013 we opened a single room with two benches and a promise:  
**No child will ever be left alone while their mother works.  
No mother will ever lose her child to preventable poverty.**  

Today Freminatos is a 4,200 m² campus serving 387 children and 241 mothers daily.  
We are not a daycare. We are not an orphanage.  
We are the bridge that keeps families intact and dignity alive.  

This document is more than bank coordinates.  
It is an invitation to become co-authors of the most important story being written in eastern Addis Ababa:  
the story of mothers who rise, children who thrive, and a community that refuses to look away.  

With gratitude and unshakable hope,  
Sister Sirgut Emikael
