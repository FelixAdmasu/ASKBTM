Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services

How can I make a donation to support ASKBTM's initiatives?

We offer multiple secure donation methods to support our mission:

  • Online Donation: Visit our secure donation portal where you can make a one-time or recurring donation using major credit/debit cards or digital wallets.
  • Bank Transfer: You can directly transfer funds to our official bank account (account details available upon request).
  • In-Person: Visit our office during working hours to make a donation in person.
  • Check/Money Order: Mail your contribution to our headquarters.

All donations are tax-deductible to the fullest extent allowed by law, and you'll receive a receipt for your records. For donations above $5,000, we offer the opportunity to direct your contribution to specific programs or initiatives.

What volunteer opportunities are available at ASKBTM?

We offer diverse volunteer opportunities across multiple departments:

  • Program Support: Assist with our educational and community outreach programs.
  • Professional Services: Offer your expertise in areas like marketing, IT, or legal services.
  • Event Volunteers: Help organize and run our fundraising and community events.
  • Administrative Support: Assist with office tasks, data entry, and donor communications.
  • Field Work: Participate in our on-ground initiatives and community development projects.

Our volunteer program includes orientation, training, and ongoing support. We require background checks for certain positions. Minimum time commitments vary by role, starting from 4 hours per month. Visit our Volunteer page to view current openings and submit an application.

How does ASKBTM utilize donations and ensure financial transparency?

At ASKBTM, we are committed to financial transparency and accountability:

Financial Allocation:

  • 80% of funds directly support our programs and services
  • 12% goes to administrative costs
  • 8% is allocated to fundraising efforts

Our Commitment:

  • Annual financial audits conducted by independent firms
  • Detailed annual reports available on our website
  • Regular updates to donors about the impact of their contributions
  • Compliance with all local and international financial regulations

We encourage donors to review our annual reports and financial statements available in the 'About Us' section of our website. For specific inquiries about our financials, please contact our Finance Department at finance@askbtm.org.

What are the requirements and process for becoming a corporate partner?

We welcome partnerships with corporations that share our vision for community development. Our corporate partnership program offers several engagement levels:

Partnership Tiers:

  • Bronze Partner: Annual contribution of $5,000-$10,000
  • Silver Partner: Annual contribution of $10,001-$25,000
  • Gold Partner: Annual contribution of $25,001-$50,000
  • Platinum Partner: Annual contribution of $50,001+

Benefits Include:

  • Brand visibility on our website and marketing materials
  • Employee engagement opportunities
  • Customized impact reports
  • Exclusive event invitations
  • Media recognition

To begin the partnership process, please contact our Corporate Relations team at partnerships@askbtm.org. We'll schedule an initial consultation to discuss alignment of values, partnership goals, and customized engagement opportunities.
