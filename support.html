Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers on donations, financial audits, volunteer onboarding, and community operations.

We provide multiple secure donation options suited for both local supporters in Ethiopia and international donors:

  • Local Bank Transfer: Direct deposit to our Commercial Bank of Ethiopia (CBE) account: 1000 488 291 034.
  • Telebirr: Quick mobile transfer to +251 911 89 27 87.
  • In-Person Delivery: Supplies (food, clothing, educational toys, hygiene packs) can be brought directly to our Legatafo campus.

Every single contribution is acknowledged with an official receipt and financial report.

We welcome volunteers from all walks of life. Core roles include:

  • Daytime Classroom Helpers: Assisting educators with early literacy, arts, and games.
  • Meal Preparation & Distribution: Helping our kitchen staff serve nutritious hot meals twice a day.
  • Skills Workshops for Mothers: Sharing vocational crafts, business basics, or tailoring skills.
  • Professional Support: Photography, medical/dental checkup volunteering, and translation.

Our daytime center runs from Monday through Friday, 8:30 AM to 5:30 PM EAT. Children are welcomed in the morning, receive breakfast, educational play, lunch, afternoon naps, and creative activities before mothers return after work.

If you wish to visit our Legatafo center, please email or call us at least 24 hours in advance so our staff can prepare a safe, organized tour for you.

Over 82% of all resources go directly toward child nutrition, learning supplies, and staff care. 10% is used for facility maintenance and utility costs, and 8% for community outreach.

We publish an annual audited report detailing fund usage, donor contributions, and child enrollment growth, which is available upon request.

Yes! We collaborate with local businesses, private schools, and international non-profits for sponsorships, supply drives, and employee volunteer days.
